Help

Manage your bottles

In Bar, choose + Bottle to add a bottle. Tap its name to edit the details or its status to update stock. Planned purchases appear under Shopping list.

Find and save a cocktail

In Recipes, open a card for measurements and method. Tap the heart to put it under Saved. Open + Cocktail to choose from the library or create a custom recipe.

Save and share menus

In Menu, save a named copy when you want to reuse the current lineup. Load a copy to work on it as a draft. Loading gives you a short Undo, and the named copy stays available.

Publish creates a guest link and QR code. If you edit the draft, publish and share a fresh link for those changes.

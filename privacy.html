Privacy

Browser storage

Your inventory, custom recipes, saved cocktails, and menu drafts are stored in this browser at this website address. Clearing site data removes them. Session storage remembers whether you’ve seen the opening animation in the current tab.

Guest menus

A published link includes the guest title, message, selected drinks, and any custom tasting notes and ingredient names shown on the menu. Anyone with the link or its QR code can open or forward it. A new publication creates a fresh link. Earlier links keep their original menu data.

Cookies, analytics, and hosting

The app does not set cookies or run analytics or advertising scripts. The beta is hosted on GitHub Pages. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details about its processing.

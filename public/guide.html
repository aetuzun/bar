The Home · Bar

Make a good drink at home

The Home Bar starts with the bottles you already have. Save a recipe worth making again, put together a menu for friends, and give everyone one link to open at the table.

It’s a small tool for the way people actually host. Use it for a single drink on a weeknight or a short menu for a roomful of friends.

Open the bar · See a sample guest menu

Help

  • In Bar, use + to add a bottle. Tap its name to edit details or tap its status to update stock.
  • In Recipes, swipe through the cards, tap a cocktail for its method, or tap its heart to shortlist it under Saved. Open + Cocktail and scroll to Create a custom cocktail to add your own.
  • In Menu, use Saved menus to keep copies of drafts. Publish creates a shareable version with a guest link and QR code. Publish and share a fresh link after you make changes.

Beta privacy

Your inventory, custom recipes, favorites, and saved menu drafts are stored in this browser. Clearing site data removes them. They are specific to this browser and website address.

The app uses browser storage for these features and session storage to remember whether you’ve seen the opening animation in this tab. It does not set cookies or run analytics or advertising scripts.

A published menu link contains the guest menu data, including the title, footer, drinks, and any custom drink tasting notes or ingredient names that appear on the menu. Anyone with the link or its QR code can open or forward it. Share a new link for an updated menu. Previously shared links keep their original data.

The beta uses GitHub Pages for hosting. See GitHub’s privacy statement for information about its processing.
